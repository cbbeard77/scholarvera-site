In pilot studies

AI changed the question.

Faculty increasingly need to understand not just what a student submitted, but how the work came together. WriteSight provides useful information about that process, without reducing it to an opaque score or automated accusation.

  • Writing-process information captured as a document is written.
  • A concise faculty view that turns that record into something readable at a glance.
  • No integrity score. It surfaces what may be worth discussing, and leaves the judgment to the educator.
  • Privacy by design. It records how the writing happened, not a copy of the writing itself.

Why ScholarVera

i.

Teach, don't fix

Name what is wrong and why, then leave the change to the writer. The intellectual work stays with the person doing it.

ii.

Process, not verdicts

Give students and faculty information they can interpret. No automated accusations, no integrity scores, no black boxes.

iii.

Privacy by design

Collect only what a tool actually needs, and never turn a student's writing into a data asset. Restraint is built in, not promised.

No cookies. No trackers. No surveillance.
