Available now

Formatting is only half the problem.

InForm is an APA 7 checker that lives inside Microsoft Word. It does not just ask whether a reference looks right. It checks whether the source behind it can actually be found.

  • APA 7 guidance that names each issue and explains why it matters.
  • Citation and reference matching, so in-text citations and the reference list stay in agreement.
  • Source verification that checks cited works against the scholarly record and flags what cannot be found.
  • Teach, don't fix. It names exactly what needs to change and leaves the writing itself alone.

In pilot studies

The other half of the question.

InForm can tell you a source is real. It cannot tell you how the paper came together. WriteSight records the writing process inside the document itself, then gives faculty a readable view of it, without reducing any of it to a score or an accusation.

  • Writing-process information captured as a document is written.
  • A concise faculty view that turns that record into something readable at a glance.
  • No integrity score. It surfaces what may be worth discussing, and leaves the judgment to the educator.
  • Privacy by design. It records how the writing happened, not a copy of the writing itself.
  • An open roadmap. What WriteSight becomes next is being worked out with the faculty piloting it.

Why ScholarVera
